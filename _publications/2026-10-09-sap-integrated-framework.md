This paper investigates how Classification and Regression Trees (CART) can support predictive process capability monitoring in large-scale logistics operations. It presents an SAP-oriented analytics framework that connects logistics data analysis, process capability assessment, and interpretable decision rules to support operational monitoring and decision-making.

The paper focuses on the potential integration of predictive analytics into enterprise logistics workflows. The GitHub repository provides access to the paper and its supporting research materials.
